\# Starter Roster \& 6-Slot Ability Framework



\## Symmetrical Vertical Slice Rule

All 4 starter archetypes share \*\*identical baseline frame data, damage, range, and cooldowns\*\* across Slots 1–4. This establishes a neutral, fair benchmark before introducing asymmetric strengths, counters, and wild builds.



\## The 4 Starter Archetypes

1\. \*\*Fighter:\*\* Frontline brawler (sword/shield visuals, thrown rock/axe projectile).

2\. \*\*Wizard:\*\* Arcane spellcaster (staff visuals, blue firebolt projectile).

3\. \*\*Rogue:\*\* Agile trickster (dagger visuals, throwing knife projectile).

4\. \*\*Ranger:\*\* Mid-range marksman (bow visuals, standard arrow projectile).



\## The 6 Move Slots

\* \*\*Slot 1 — Quick Attack:\*\* Fast close-range check (low startup, low knockback).

\* \*\*Slot 2 — Heavy Launcher:\*\* Slower wind-up with high knockback to secure KOs.

\* \*\*Slot 3 — Standard Projectile:\*\* Identical speed, range, and cooldown across all 4 archetypes.

\* \*\*Slot 4 — Recovery / Mobility:\*\* Universal directional air-burst to return to stage ledges.

\* \*\*Slot 5 — Character Special:\*\* Signature class mechanic (medium-long cooldown, reserved for Steven's custom designs).

\* \*\*Slot 6 — Character Ultimate:\*\* High-impact, long-cooldown game-changer (reserved for Steven's custom designs).



\## Core Defense \& Health

\* \*\*Health / Knockback:\*\* Starts with a clean HP pool system, engineered inside `CombatRules.gd` so we can easily toggle or experiment with Smash-style percentage scaling later.

\* \*\*Basic Block:\*\* Dedicated guard state that reduces incoming damage and knockback, built to expand into parries and shield-breaks.
